Virtual Urogynaecology Consultation (Video)

Excellent ★★★★★ 4.98/5 Doctify Rating (188 verified reviews)

I offer private video consultations for women who want specialist advice about prolapse, urinary incontinence, overactive bladder or other pelvic floor and gynaecological symptoms without travelling to clinic. I am a consultant urogynaecologist with RCOG subspecialty accreditation and FRCOG, based at Spire Claremont Hospital and Circle Thornbury Hospital in Sheffield. Video consultations are suitable for initial assessment, second opinions and follow-up; if an examination proves necessary, I will explain this and arrange a face-to-face appointment. No GP referral is required.

Conditions I Can Assess by Video

A video consultation may be right for you if you'd like advice about:

  • Vaginal or pelvic organ prolapse
  • Urinary incontinence
  • Overactive bladder and urinary urgency
  • Recurrent urinary tract infections
  • Pelvic floor symptoms following childbirth
  • Perineal trauma, including previous third- or fourth-degree tears
  • Pelvic floor pain or discomfort
  • Problems following previous prolapse or continence surgery
  • Menopausal symptoms affecting the bladder or vagina
  • A second opinion on diagnosis, investigations or treatment
  • Follow-up after previous treatment or surgery

What Happens During a Video Consultation?

Your consultation runs much like a face-to-face appointment — it simply takes place securely by video. I'll go through:

  • When your symptoms started
  • How they're affecting your daily life
  • Previous pregnancies and childbirth
  • Previous operations or treatments
  • Your bladder, bowel and pelvic floor symptoms
  • Current medication and relevant medical conditions
  • Previous scans, investigations or clinic letters
  • Your treatment preferences and any concerns

You'll have time to ask questions and go through the treatment options available to you.

How the Video Consultation Works

Video consultations are available at a time that suits you, including evenings and weekends where availability allows. You can join from your mobile, tablet or desktop using whichever platform is easiest for you — FaceTime, WhatsApp Video, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet — with nothing to download or install.

Consultations are available UK-wide and, where appropriate, from abroad — useful if you need advice while travelling or living outside Sheffield.

To book, contact my secretary Lauren Hudson at admin@swatijha.com or +44 7990 251036. She will confirm availability and take payment details before the appointment.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, do not wait for a video consultation — call 999 or attend your nearest A&E.

Fees, Payment and Cancellation

The fee for a video consultation is £350 for a new patient assessment and £200 for a follow-up.

Payment is taken in advance and your appointment is confirmed once payment is received.

Due to unforseen circumstances, I may have to cancel your scheduled appointment but will rearrange it within 24 hours or will refund the money to your bank account.

Can Everything Be Diagnosed by Video?

Many pelvic floor problems can be assessed initially through a detailed clinical history alone. Some, though, need a physical or pelvic examination before I can give a definite diagnosis or treatment recommendation.

If examination turns out to be necessary, I'll explain this during the consultation and arrange a face-to-face appointment at Spire Claremont or Circle Thornbury.

This is why a video consultation works particularly well as a first assessment, a follow-up, or a specialist second opinion.

Before Your Appointment

Where you can, please have ready:

  • A list of your current medications
  • Details of previous operations
  • Previous clinic letters
  • Scan or investigation reports
  • Details of treatments you've already tried

Join from a quiet, private space with a reliable internet connection.

After Your Consultation

You'll leave the appointment with a clear plan for what happens next. Depending on your symptoms, this may include:

  • Reassurance and lifestyle advice
  • Referral for pelvic floor physiotherapy
  • Medication
  • Further investigations
  • A face-to-face examination
  • A discussion of surgical and non-surgical treatment options
  • A follow-up consultation

Where appropriate, I'll also write to your GP.

Is a Video Consultation Right for Me?

A virtual consultation is particularly useful if you:

  • Live some distance from Sheffield
  • Have difficulty travelling
  • Want an initial specialist opinion before committing to a clinic visit
  • Need a follow-up appointment
  • Would like a second opinion
  • Want to discuss treatment options before deciding whether to attend in person

If examination turns out to be necessary, I'll arrange this separately.

Frequently Asked Questions — Video Consultation

Do I need a GP referral for a video consultation?

No. You can book a private video consultation with me directly, without a GP referral. Contact my secretary Lauren Hudson at admin@swatijha.com or call 07990 251036.

Can prolapse or incontinence be diagnosed without an examination?

Often, yes, at least as a first step. A detailed clinical history goes a long way for most pelvic floor problems. Some conditions do need a physical or pelvic examination before I can confirm a diagnosis or recommend treatment — if that applies to you, I'll explain this during the video consultation and arrange a face-to-face appointment.

How do I join the video consultation?

You'll receive a secure video link ahead of your appointment, or we can use FaceTime, WhatsApp Video, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet — whichever suits you best. There's nothing to download in advance. Join from a quiet, private place with a reliable internet connection at the time booked.

Can I book a video consultation from outside the UK?

Yes. Video consultations are available UK-wide and, where appropriate, from abroad — useful if you need advice while travelling or living outside Sheffield.

Is a video consultation covered by my insurance?

Most major UK insurers cover specialist consultations, including video appointments, subject to your policy terms. I accept Aviva, AXA Health, BUPA, BUPA Fee Assured, Cigna, Vitality, WPA, Freedom Health, The Exeter, Benenden Health, Healix, Saga and Simply Health. Please confirm cover with your insurer before booking. Self-pay patients are also welcome.

What happens if I need a physical examination after a video consultation?

I'll explain why an examination is needed and arrange a face-to-face appointment at Spire Claremont Hospital or Circle Thornbury Hospital. Nothing about the video consultation is wasted — it forms part of your assessment and saves time at the in-person visit.

Can I get a second opinion by video?

Yes. A video consultation is a convenient way to get a specialist second opinion on a diagnosis, investigation results or a proposed treatment plan, without travelling to Sheffield.

Is my video consultation private and secure?

Yes. Video consultations run on a secure platform, and the same confidentiality applies as to any clinic appointment.

How much does a private video consultation cost?

The fee is £350 for a new patient assessment and £200 for a follow-up. Payment is taken in advance and your appointment is confirmed once payment is received.
